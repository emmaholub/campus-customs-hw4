# Campus Customs design updates

- Yale-blue palette and pale blue surfaces establish a recognizable campus-store identity.
- Display-style headings create a more editorial, premium feeling than a default app layout.
- Product cards use consistent image proportions, price emphasis, and hover lift so the catalogue is easy to scan.
- Responsive grids keep products usable on smaller screens.
- The chat panel now has a compact visual hierarchy and product cards that link directly to detail pages.
- Search/filter controls reduce the effort needed to find a hoodie, gift, color, or team item.
- Subtle motion is limited to useful hover feedback so browsing feels polished without becoming distracting.
